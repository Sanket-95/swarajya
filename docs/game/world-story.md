## How we separate history from fiction
| Layer | What it contains | Who may change it |
|---|---|---|
| **History** | Real people, places, dates and events | Only after checking reliable sources; record sources in the chapter doc |
| **Historical colour** | Clothing, weapons, architecture, food, festivals, daily life | Researched; when unsure keep it generic and plausible |
| **Fiction** | The player Mavala, his family, friends, village, side quests, invented minor characters | Free, as long as it never contradicts or diminishes history and never puts words into revered figures' mouths that are not appropriate |

Each mission design doc has a **history-vs-fiction note** that lists which elements are which.

- **Time:** 17th century, roughly the 1640s–1680, the life of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj (born at Shivneri, 1630; coronation at Raigad, 1674). TODO(Sanket): verify, and decide which years the game covers.

- **Powers of the time:** the Adilshahi sultanate of Bijapur, the Mughal empire, the Siddis of Janjira, and European trading companies on the coast. In this game all conflict is shown as **political/military**, never religious or community hatred. Enemies are "soldiers of the opposing army".

## Tone
- Heroic, sincere, grounded. Courage, loyalty, discipline, sacrifice, love of the land.
- No gore; stylised hit feedback only.
- Sacrifices of revered figures are told through narration, painting-style stills, music and tribute screens, never shown graphically and never scored.
- Humour only in the fictional, everyday-life parts, never involving revered figures.
