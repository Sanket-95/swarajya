## History (outline; verify)
1. **The siege (1660).** After the events at Pratapgad (1659), Shivaji Maharaj was at **Panhala** fort (near Kolhapur). An Adilshahi army commanded by **Siddi Jauhar** besieged Panhala, reportedly from around March 1660. The siege lasted for months into the monsoon. 2. **The plan to escape.** With the siege unbroken, a plan was made to leave Panhala secretly at night and reach **Vishalgad**, a fort roughly 60+ km away. Sources commonly describe negotiations used as a distraction. TODO(Sanket): verify.
3. **Shiva Kashid.** Accounts describe **Shiva Kashid**, who resembled Shivaji Maharaj, going out in a second palanquin as a decoy; he was captured and gave his life. TODO(Sanket): verify; decide with the owner whether Shiva Kashid is treated as a revered figure (recommended: yes).
4. **The night escape (July 1660).** On a stormy monsoon night, commonly given as **12–13 July 1660** (Ashadh Pournima / Guru Pournima in some accounts), Shivaji Maharaj left Panhala with a small force of Mavale through difficult terrain. TODO(Sanket): verify date and route.
5. **Ghod Khind (Pavan Khind).** Pursued by enemy forces, the group reached a narrow pass, **Ghod Khind**. **Baji Prabhu Deshpande** stayed behind with a few hundred Mavale (commonly given as ~300, many of the Bandal company) to hold the pass so that Shivaji Maharaj could reach Vishalgad. 6. **The signal.** It was agreed that cannon shots from Vishalgad would signal Shivaji Maharaj's safe arrival. Baji Prabhu held the pass until the signal was heard, and gave his life. The pass was afterwards called **Pavan Khind** ("sacred pass"). TODO(Sanket): verify.

## How the game will present it (design intent, not history)
- The player is a fictional Mavala in the escape party, then among the men who stay at Ghod Khind with Baji Prabhu Deshpande.
- Shivaji Maharaj, Baji Prabhu Deshpande and Shiva Kashid are **revered**: AI-controlled, dignified, untouchable, no graphic death.
- Shiva Kashid's sacrifice and Baji Prabhu's sacrifice are told through **narration, painting-style stills, music and a tribute screen**, with no score or reward.
- Mission failure fades out with neutral text and retries; it never shows harm to a revered figure.
